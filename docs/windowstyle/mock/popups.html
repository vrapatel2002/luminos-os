Keyboard

Empty stateInside the list card: a 56 px tonal disc with the icon, a 16 px title, one helpful sentence, and the next step as a tonal button. Never an empty black box.
Off vs onLayouts off: explains that the system layout is used and offers to turn it on. On but empty: offers "Add layout". Flip the switch to see both, then add two layouts.
Where it livesThe text belongs to the Keyboard page's own code (kcm_keyboard). The card, disc and button styling are global. Other pages with empty lists get the same look from kcmutils + Kirigami PlaceholderMessage.

Info bars global

Tonal card r16 with a leading icon, the message, one text action and a round close button. Four tones: info (#45455c), positive, warning, error. Slides in over 180 ms. When pinned to the top of a page (like "No pointer device found") it keeps the page's side margins and rounds only the bottom corners. kirigami: InlineMessage

Tooltips global

Plain: inverse surface (#e5e1e7 on #313034), r8, 12.5 px, shortcut in grey, shows after 500 ms, 120 ms fade. Rich (help text): r12 on #353438 with a title line, max 260 px wide. darkly: PE_PanelTipLabel · qqc2: ToolTip

Scroll bars global

No track, no arrows. A 4 px pill at 45 % outline that grows to 8 px and 75 % when the pointer is over the list. Rounded ends, 4 px from the edge. Fades out 1.5 s after scrolling stops (QML views). darkly: CC_ScrollBar · qqc2: ScrollBar
