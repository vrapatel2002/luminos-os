Luminos Style Tuner — prototype

Drag a bar left or right (or click it and use the arrow keys). Double-click resets it; the small tick marks today's value. The window on the right stands in for your real System Settings: in the built version the bars change that app live, along with Dolphin, Discover and every other KDE app.

Style Tuner

Changes apply the moment you move a bar and are kept in ~/.config/luminos/style.json. Bake writes them into the KDE code as the new defaults.
