Connected devices

The names, so we can talk about them

Nav itemA page in the left list: icon disc, label, grey description. Its corners depend on where it sits in its category.
Category groupNav items that belong together (Network, Connected devices, Audio). 4 px gaps inside, 16 px between groups.
Connected cornersFirst item 28 px on top, last 28 px on the bottom, everything that touches a neighbour 4 px. Read together, they look like one card.
Selected pillThe current nav item turns #45455c and takes 32 px on all four corners. The change animates.
Press squashWhile a row is held down, every corner drops to 12 px and a ripple spreads from your finger.
Icon discRound backing behind each icon. Soft grey normally, bright lavender with a dark icon when selected or connected.
ConnectedRectThe same corner rule on the page side, with first/last switches: Bluetooth (first), device list, Pair new device (last).
ToggleRow / ItemListA ConnectedRect holding a switch, or a list of transparent rows. Rows inside a list keep 4 px corners.
StyledSwitchPill track with a round thumb. The ✗ morphs into a ✓ when flipped, and the thumb stretches while pressed.
Frame and holeThe window is one solid shape with the page cut out of it, which is why the right side looks recessed.
