## Best guesses for the "snappier" feel (unproven)
- The suspend/resume cycles themselves: each resume re-initialises the iGPU (amdgpu SMU/DMUB), KWin's outputs, Wi-Fi
  and re-asserts luminos-power PPT/EPP — any slowly-degrading state (GPU clocks, compositor, a leaking app) gets a reset.
- To test: note responsiveness, run one 30 s test sleep (`/var/tmp/sleeptest/run.sh 30`), compare. If it repeats,
  something degrades over uptime and a resume clears it — that's a bug worth hunting, not a feature.

## Findings from the tests (feed the strong-sleep build)
- Deep sleep works: 34.9 s of 40 s in hardware sleep (amd_pmc S0ix residency).
- Entry is slow by 2.4-4.5 s: the NVIDIA PM notifier (nv_pm_notifier → nv_set_system_power_state) WAKES the sleeping
  RTX 4050 out of D3cold (rpm_resume on 0000:01:00.0 at suspend, ~2 s to come up) just to suspend it again.
  Resume pays it again: 0.79 s. Cause: NVreg_UseKernelSuspendNotifiers=1 + PreserveVideoMemoryAllocations=1
  (/usr/lib/modprobe.d/nvidia-sleep.conf, nvidia-utils package defaults).
- First sleep attempt often aborts ("queueing ieee80211 work while going to suspend" + "Some devices failed to suspend");
  empty SD reader mmc0 errors after every resume. luminos-lid fires once and never retries.
- Kernel resume ≈ 1.8 s; slowest devices: PCIe port 00:01.1 (the dGPU's bridge) 0.6 s, amdgpu 0.42 s, nvme 0.48 s on entry.
